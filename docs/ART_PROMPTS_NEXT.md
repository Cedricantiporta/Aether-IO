## (1) STYLE — paste first, every time
```
Style: each icon is ONE small 3D object rendered like a handmade miniature toy / clay-and-vinyl figurine, exactly
like the item icons in The Legend of Zelda: Echoes of Wisdom (Nintendo Switch). Soft rounded chunky shapes, gentle
bevels, matte clay / painted-wood / soft-plastic materials with subtle surface texture, soft studio lighting from the
top-left, soft ambient occlusion, a faint soft grey contact shadow directly under the object. Three-quarter view,
slightly from above, object centered and filling about 75% of its cell. Warm, cheerful, natural colours; magical
effects (fire, ice, light, wind, lightning, shadow) are modelled as soft solid sculpted shapes attached to the object,
like toy VFX, never glowing neon or 2D strokes. Cute and charming, simple readable silhouette at 40 px. No outlines,
no 2D painting, no realism, no text, no letters, no numbers, no frames, no scenery.
```

## (2) LAYOUT — paste second, every time
```
Layout: one 1024x1024 image, a 4x4 grid of 16 separate objects, each centered in its own equal square cell.
Background of the WHOLE image: perfectly flat solid pure magenta (#FF00FF), no gradients, no texture, no floor,
no frames, no gutters. Each object fills about 75% of its cell, fully inside its cell, never touching or crossing
into another cell. Do NOT use magenta, pink or pink-purple inside the objects, and nothing see-through (no glass
bubbles showing the background). Same camera angle, lighting, size and material style for all 16.
Order: left to right, top to bottom, exactly as listed.
```
